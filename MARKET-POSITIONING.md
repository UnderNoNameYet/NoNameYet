# RebuttalKit market positioning

## Blunt market reality

Publishing a useful app does not create demand. Generic response writing is inexpensive because merchants can use ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or processor-provided tools. Stripe Smart Disputes can assemble and submit eligible evidence automatically, and Shopify Payments automates many responses.

RebuttalKit must therefore avoid the claim that it is simply a better AI chargeback writer.

## The narrow wedge

> A local-first evidence workspace and fixed-fee human clarity review for high-value service disputes where delivery proof lives outside the payment platform.

The product is strongest when:

- the seller delivered an intangible or custom service;
- the disputed amount is approximately $750–$5,000;
- evidence spans a proposal, invoice, meeting, approval, messages, and shared files;
- the case is manual, ineligible for platform automation, or unusually complex;
- the seller wants an independent check but retains final submission control.

Examples include agencies, consultants, coaches, freelancers, implementation specialists, software studios, and other independent service businesses.

## Do not lead with

- “AI-generated rebuttal”;
- generic Shopify shipment disputes;
- automatic submission;
- win-rate or recovery claims;
- replacing Stripe, Shopify, PayPal, a lawyer, or a recovery platform;
- a subscription for an occasional problem.

## Message hierarchy

1. **Problem:** the delivery story is spread across tools and difficult to verify quickly.
2. **Free value:** organize the exact reason, deadline, chronology, and numbered records locally.
3. **Proof:** show the fictional before/after review and exception list.
4. **Paid value:** a second chronology, consistency, and evidence-order pass for $69.
5. **Trust:** no processor login, no automatic upload, customer-controlled handoff, explicit deletion date.
6. **Boundary:** organization and clarity only; the customer verifies and submits.

## Why a customer might pay

For a $1,500 dispute, $69 can be a reasonable fixed cost if it saves several hours, catches a contradiction, and makes the record easier to inspect. For a $150 dispute, the same fee is usually poor economics. The paid offer should not target low-value cases even though the free workspace can still help them.

## Competitive posture

### Built-in automation

Recommend it when it is eligible and complete. RebuttalKit is complementary when important evidence exists outside platform data or the merchant wants to inspect the record before submission.

### Generic AI

Generic AI can rewrite prose but may not enforce evidence traceability, preserve contradictions, understand processor-specific constraints, or provide a controlled customer-data workflow. RebuttalKit should sell the evidence map and review process—not prose generation.

### Recovery services

Recovery platforms suit recurring volume and outsourced operations. RebuttalKit suits occasional, higher-value service cases where a merchant does not want an integration, subscription, or percentage fee.

## Acquisition implication

Organic discovery is unlikely to generate the first two sales in ten days. The initial channel is manually qualified introductions through:

- bookkeepers and accountants serving agencies;
- web and ecommerce consultants;
- virtual operations specialists;
- fractional finance leaders;
- agency-owner and consultant communities;
- payment specialists who encounter manual or ineligible cases.

Public replies must solve the immediate problem first and follow community promotion rules.

## Validation threshold

Do not add broad features before observing real cases.

Continue the offer if either occurs within the launch test:

- at least two customers pay; or
- at least five qualified customers request a review and the same removable objection blocks purchase.

Reposition or stop if 50 manually qualified conversations produce no compatibility checks. Treat compliments, impressions, and generic signups as weak evidence.
