How to read it

Pass does not mean “secure forever.”

A pass means the observed behavior matched the documented expectation for that check, environment, identity, and code state. Untested and out-of-scope paths remain visible instead of being silently treated as safe.

Why the evidence is redacted

Customer-facing reports should prove what was exercised without becoming a map of raw identifiers, keys, personal records, or sensitive implementation details. Full technical traces belong in the authorized engineering handoff.
