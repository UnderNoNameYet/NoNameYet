Pre-launch terms

Service boundaries and limitations

Effective August 30, 2026. These terms describe the intended MVP and do not create a customer engagement or authorize testing.

No engagement through this website

The local MVP does not accept orders, payments, targets, credentials, or testing authorization. Generating or downloading a scope brief does not create a contract.

Written authorization required

Testing may begin only after the verified system owner and TenantProof agree in writing on the target, environment, identities, schedule, methods, exclusions, and stop conditions. Do not use TenantProof materials to test a system you do not own or lack permission to assess.

Nature of the service

TenantProof is intended to verify selected multi-tenant authorization expectations and, where purchased, prepare selected remediation. It is not a comprehensive penetration test, legal review, compliance certification, or guarantee that an application has no vulnerability.

Meaning of a result

A pass applies only to the documented check, code state, environment, identity, and time of execution. A failure records observed behavior that contradicted the agreed expectation. Untested and out-of-scope areas receive no safety inference.

Customer responsibilities

A future customer must provide accurate architecture and authorization information, maintain backups, designate a technical contact, use an appropriate test environment, review proposed changes, and control deployment to production.

Pricing

Published amounts are starting prices for the stated limits, not automatic acceptance of every project. Scope, payment terms, refund terms, taxes, liability, governing law, and operator identity must be confirmed in a written service agreement before launch.

Fictional demonstration

Northstar CRM, its identities, findings, evidence, and remediation results are fictional. They are a product demonstration—not client work, a testimonial, or proof of commercial results.

Changes before launch

These pre-launch terms require legal and operational review before the service accepts a customer or payment.
