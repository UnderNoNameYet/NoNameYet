Service boundaries and limitations

Updated September 5, 2026. The $349 Boundary Verification offer is bookable through Upwork; this static website does not itself accept orders or payments.

Current booking path

The approved Upwork Project Catalog offer is the only live marketplace booking path. Upwork handles its account, messaging, contracting, payment, refund, and platform terms. Generating or downloading a scope brief on this website does not create a contract.

Booking does not authorize testing

Testing may begin only after the verified system owner and provider agree in writing on the target, environment, identities, schedule, methods, exclusions, and stop conditions. Do not use TenantBoundary materials to test a system you do not own or lack permission to assess.

Nature of the service

TenantBoundary verifies selected multi-tenant authorization expectations and provides remediation guidance within the purchased scope. It is not a comprehensive penetration test, legal review, compliance certification, or guarantee that an application has no vulnerability.

Meaning of a result

A pass applies only to the documented check, code state, environment, identity, fixture, and execution time. A failure records observed behavior that contradicted the agreed expectation. Untested and out-of-scope areas receive no safety inference.

Customer responsibilities

The customer must provide accurate architecture and authorization information, maintain backups, designate a technical contact, use an appropriate test environment, review proposed changes, and control deployment to production.

Pricing and scope

The live Upwork offer is Boundary Verification at $349 for its stated limits. The $649 Verification + Repair amount shown on this site is a starting scope, not a direct checkout. Larger, production-only, regulated, or unusually sensitive work requires separate qualification. Taxes and platform-specific payment or refund handling follow the applicable Upwork order and written scope.

Fictional demonstration

Northstar CRM, its identities, findings, evidence, and remediation results are fictional. They are a product demonstration—not client work, a testimonial, or proof of commercial results.

Direct website intake

This website remains a no-submit preview. Direct contracting or payment will not open until verified operator, contact, jurisdiction, privacy, retention, and deletion details are published.
