Pre-launch notice

Privacy and data handling

Effective August 30, 2026. This local MVP does not yet accept customers, transmit request forms, or operate a hosted checkout.

Current MVP behavior: the scope worksheet and report viewer run locally in your browser. TenantProof has not configured analytics, customer accounts, form submission, or payment processing.

Information processed locally

If you use the scope worksheet, the application details and contact fields remain in the browser unless you choose to copy or download the generated text. If you open a report file, JavaScript reads it in memory for display; the site does not upload it.

Information not requested publicly

Do not enter API keys, database passwords, service-role credentials, database exports, customer records, or other secrets into the public website.

Future operating policy

Before a public launch that accepts requests or payments, this notice must be updated with the verified operator identity, contact method, processors, retention periods, access procedures, deletion process, and applicable jurisdiction. Those details will not be invented before they exist.

Engagement data

For an approved customer engagement, access, evidence retention, deletion, and subprocessors will be documented in the written scope. The default is staging, synthetic data, least privilege, time-bounded access, and redacted reporting.

Contact

A verified operator contact will be published before the service accepts customer information. No placeholder or unowned email address is presented in this MVP.
