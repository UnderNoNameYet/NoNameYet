# RebuttalKit founder-review operations runbook

## Service boundary

One $69 founder packet review covers one accepted, active dispute and one correction round. It is an evidence-organization and writing-clarity service. It is not legal advice, accounting advice, processor representation, automatic submission, evidence creation, or an outcome guarantee.

## 1. Compatibility screening

Accept only when all are true:

- processor and reason are supported or understandable;
- deadline is normally 3–14 days away;
- records already exist;
- customer controls or is authorized to use the records;
- requested work fits the published deliverable;
- capacity supports the promised window.

Reject expired deadlines, legal interpretation, account-access requests, fabricated-evidence requests, harassment, sanctioned or unlawful activity, and cases too large or complex for the fixed scope.

Do not request documents before acceptance.

## 2. Acceptance record

Create an anonymous review ID such as `RK-2026-001`. Record:

- processor and dispute reason;
- amount range, not card information;
- evidence deadline and time zone;
- accepted scope;
- price and currency;
- hosted checkout sent time;
- secure handoff method;
- turnaround start condition;
- deletion date;
- refund boundary.

Send the checkout only after this record is complete.

## 3. Secure handoff

Preferred method: a customer-controlled folder with access limited to the published review address and an expiration or revocation date.

Never request processor credentials, card data, identity documents unrelated to the dispute, public links, social-message attachments, or GitHub uploads.

Ask the customer to use short neutral filenames and remove unnecessary sensitive data. Keep customer files out of the repository, analytics, issue tracker, and AI services without explicit informed consent.

## 4. Review passes

### Pass A — case boundary

Copy the exact reason, deadline, transaction, sale type, promised deliverable, and merchant position. Flag contradictions before drafting.

### Pass B — chronology

Order only verifiable events. Each event needs a date, neutral description, and source record. Do not infer intent.

### Pass C — evidence map

Match records to the allegation. Remove duplicates and irrelevant volume. Flag missing links rather than inventing them.

### Pass D — response

Use a short structure: position, chronology, reason-specific facts, evidence references, conclusion. Avoid emotional language, accusations, legal conclusions, and unsupported certainty.

### Pass E — packet control

Recommend numbered filenames, index order, legibility checks, and processor file constraints. The customer remains responsible for checking the live portal.

## 5. Delivery package

Deliver:

1. case map;
2. recommended evidence order and filenames;
3. revised factual response;
4. exceptions and questions;
5. final verification checklist.

State clearly that the customer must verify every fact and submit through the processor.

## 6. Correction round

The included correction round covers factual errors, missing supplied context, and clarity changes. New disputes, new evidence sets, legal analysis, account work, or a second rewrite require a new scope and must not be silently added.

## 7. Refunds

- Full refund before review work starts.
- Full refund if the promised delivery window is missed and the customer chooses not to continue.
- Completed review work is otherwise non-refundable, subject to applicable law and payment-provider rules.

Record the reason and timestamp. Never make a customer waive mandatory consumer rights.

## 8. Deletion

Delete working copies within seven days after delivery or final correction, whichever is later, unless a shorter period was agreed. Empty local trash where appropriate, revoke local sync, and send a deletion confirmation. Do not claim deletion of copies controlled by the customer or infrastructure provider.

## 9. Incident response

If private data is misdirected, exposed, or stored in an unapproved location:

1. stop intake and checkout;
2. preserve only the minimum incident record;
3. contain access;
4. notify the operator and affected customer promptly;
5. follow applicable legal and provider requirements;
6. document the fix before reopening.

## 10. Daily capacity

Never accept more work than can be delivered safely. The initial public limit is three cases per week. Lower it immediately if response time, quality, or deletion discipline slips.
