# Commercial model and payments

## Commercial objective

Validate whether a founder will pay for executed tenant-boundary evidence within ten days of public launch. The target is at least $100; the preferred signal is one full paid pilot rather than a discounted micro-sale.

No revenue, customer, testimonial, or conversion rate exists yet.

## Packages

### Boundary Verification — $349

Deliverables:

- up to 12 named tables
- up to 3 application roles
- agreed read/write matrix
- redacted report
- remediation guidance
- one retest

Commercial logic: one sale clears the experiment target and is small enough for a founder making a launch decision.

### Verification + Repair — $649

Deliverables:

- up to 25 named tables
- up to 3 roles
- selected functions
- one storage bucket
- SQL/RLS migrations
- reviewable pull request
- regression checks
- one retest

Recommended milestones: $324.50 at authorized start and $324.50 at delivery/accepted retest, or one agreed $649 invoice when platform terms make that clearer.

### Manual quote

Use for broader, regulated, production-only, incident, or high-complexity scope. Do not stretch fixed packages to win work that cannot be delivered safely.

## Pricing principles

- price the decision artifact and risk reduction, not hours alone
- show scope ceilings
- do not invent a discount anchor
- do not undercut to compete with $50 automated/generic audits
- do not claim “premium” without evidence quality and service discipline
- revise only from actual objections, conversion, and fulfillment time

## Initial payment workflow

1. qualify non-sensitive request
2. agree expectation matrix and environment
3. issue SOW and authorization
4. confirm operator/legal identity, jurisdiction, tax/refund terms
5. issue processor-hosted invoice
6. receive agreed milestone
7. begin only after payment/authorization conditions are met
8. deliver/retest
9. settle remaining milestone
10. send final receipt and closeout

Recommended processor: Stripe invoices when an operator-owned account exists. Upwork escrow is acceptable for buyers sourced there and may reduce initial trust friction. Never route around platform rules.

## Why no unrestricted checkout

A buy-now button could accept:

- unauthorized targets
- production-only scopes
- regulated/high-risk projects
- scopes above package ceilings
- work during unavailable capacity

Qualification protects both sides. Payment remains closed until scope is valid.

## Refund and cancellation model

Canonical draft: `operations/payment-refund-workflow.md`.

Recommended principles:

- before access/work: refund minus non-recoverable processor fees where lawful and disclosed
- after scoped work begins: refund reflects completed milestone and committed work
- unauthorized or unsafe scope: stop; refund unearned portion
- operator inability to deliver: prompt refund of unearned amount
- new requirements: written change order, not hidden extra work
- customer production deployment is outside acceptance unless explicitly included

Final terms require verified jurisdiction and appropriate legal review.

## Unit-economics worksheet

For every paid pilot record:

| Metric | Purpose |
|---|---|
| gross price | revenue signal |
| processor/platform fee | channel cost |
| qualification + scope hours | sales cost |
| execution hours | delivery cost |
| remediation hours | package margin |
| reporting/retest hours | hidden labor |
| acquisition hours/cost | channel efficiency |
| refund/revision cost | promise quality |

Do not automate pricing until at least three comparable paid engagements exist.

## Capacity

Initial maximum: one active pilot. Increase only after the first delivery proves time, tool, and support assumptions.

## Payment activation blockers

- legal contracting identity
- jurisdiction
- owned business contact
- owned processor/account
- accurate public privacy/terms
- tax handling
- refund/cancellation language
- secure access exchange
- delivery capacity
- live site/intake monitoring

## Future commercial paths — evidence-gated

### Retainer/regression run

Consider after buyers request reruns across releases. Possible scope: monthly matrix execution against agreed staging fixtures. Do not promise continuous monitoring without infrastructure.

### Team/agency package

Consider after agencies bring repeated client apps. Must preserve per-client authorization and evidence separation.

### Customer CI template

Potential one-time add-on for regression checks inside the customer repository. Price only after delivery effort is known.

### SaaS subscription

Not before recurring demand, secure multi-customer architecture, report access control, audit logging, and legal/privacy capacity are proven.

## Commercial truth rules

- $100 in ten days is an experiment target
- recent public budgets indicate willingness, not guaranteed demand
- sample findings are fictional
- starting prices are not universal acceptance
- no artificial scarcity
- no fake “spots remaining”
- no fabricated money-back guarantee
- no testimonial/case study without exact approval
