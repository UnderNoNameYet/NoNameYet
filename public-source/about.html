WHY WE MADE IT

The processor sends one label.
The business has twenty fragments.

A deadline lands beside a reason code. The useful facts are somewhere else: an invoice, a signed scope, a calendar record, a delivery message, an access log. The difficult part is not producing more language. It is reconstructing the transaction without losing the thread.

WHAT THE APP IS

An evidence workstation—
not a verdict machine.

01

It establishes the case

Reason, deadline, processor wording, amount, transaction, and sale type are recorded before the response begins.

02

It changes the evidence checklist

The records that matter depend on the allegation and whether the sale was physical, digital, recurring, or a service.

03

It resolves chronology before prose

Dated events expose gaps and contradictions while there is still time to fix the packet.

04

It assembles one exportable record

The factual response, evidence index, and final checks all refer to the same user-verified facts.

THE PRODUCT OATH / FIVE CONSTRAINTS

Useful because of what it refuses to do.

  1. 01Record before argument.

    The chronology earns priority over persuasive filler.

  2. 02Expose missing proof.

    A visible gap is safer than a confident invention.

  3. 03Keep case data local.

    No RebuttalKit case-data server exists in this beta.

  4. 04Leave the final decision to the user.

    You verify, export, and submit through the live portal.

  5. 05Never sell certainty.

    Readiness measures organization, not the chance of winning.
