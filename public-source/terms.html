An organization tool—not a promise.

Use RebuttalKit to arrange existing records into a response packet. You remain responsible for the facts, evidence, deadline, and final submission.

Product boundary

Beta availability

RebuttalKit is currently provided without charge for evaluation. Features may change, and the beta may be interrupted or withdrawn. No payment checkout or paid review service is active.

Your responsibility

You must verify every statement, date, amount, identifier, and evidence reference. Check the live processor portal for its current deadline, reason wording, accepted evidence, file types, size limits, and submission requirements. Card-network and processor rules can change.

Acceptable use

Use the product only with records you are permitted to process. Do not fabricate, alter, backdate, mislabel, or conceal evidence. Do not use the product to impersonate a customer, evade lawful obligations, or submit information you know is false.

No outcome guarantee

The readiness score measures completeness and organization inside the tool. It is not a probability, legal assessment, or prediction. Banks, issuers, processors, and card networks independently decide disputes.

Local data and backups

The beta stores case information in the browser. You are responsible for device security and backups. Export a JSON copy before clearing browser data. The product cannot recover deleted local data.

Paid service boundary

If a reviewed-packet service is approved later, it will require separate published scope, price, turnaround, secure intake, retention, refund, and business identity information. The proposed founder review shown on the product page is not currently available for purchase.

Use at your own risk

The beta is provided as available without a promise that it is error-free, uninterrupted, or suitable for a particular dispute. To the extent allowed by applicable law, responsibility for using or relying on the generated material remains with the user.
