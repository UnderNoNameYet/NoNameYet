01 / RESPONSE REVISION

Shorter because the records
do more of the work.

BEFOREUNREVIEWED DRAFT

I cannot believe this customer disputed the charge after all the work we did. We had many calls and messages and they were clearly happy with everything. This is obviously friendly fraud and they are trying to steal our work. We completed the strategy project exactly as promised and sent all the files, so the dispute should be rejected immediately.

01

Accuses the customer without a record proving intent.

02

Says “many calls and messages” but does not identify dates.

03

Claims every deliverable was sent without mapping the evidence.

04

Asks for an outcome instead of making the chronology easy to inspect.

AFTERFOUNDER-REVIEW REVISION

The records show that the fixed-scope strategy service was delivered according to the accepted proposal.

The customer accepted the scope on 31 July 2026 [E-01] and paid invoice INV-2087 on 1 August 2026 [E-02]. The scheduled workshop took place on 7 August for 58 minutes [E-03]. The written strategy brief was sent after the workshop, and the customer acknowledged receiving it on 9 August [E-04].

These dated records show delivery of both components listed in the accepted scope. The merchant asks that the issuer review the attached scope, payment, attendance, delivery, and acknowledgment records.

03 / EXCEPTION LIST

Open questions stay visible.

RESOLVE

Delivery file is not indexed

The email that attached the final brief should be included or the response should avoid implying the attachment itself is already proven.

VERIFY

Portal evidence limits

Confirm current file types, combined size, and whether multiple communications must be consolidated before submission.

REMOVE

Unsupported intent claim

“Friendly fraud” describes motive. The supplied records establish delivery, not the customer’s reason for disputing.

The customer decides.
