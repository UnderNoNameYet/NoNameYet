 BOUNDARIES
Clear before checkout.

      
What about Stripe Smart Disputes or Shopify automation?+
Use built-in automation when it is eligible and already contains the evidence needed. RebuttalKit is for manual or ineligible cases, evidence that lives outside the platform, or an independent clarity check before you decide what to submit.
Is this legal advice?+
No. The review is an organization and clarity service based only on records you provide. It does not interpret law, represent you, or predict the issuer’s decision.
Do you submit the dispute?+
No. We never request processor credentials. You verify and submit the final material through the current processor portal.
When do I pay?+
Only after the compatibility request is accepted and the scope, turnaround, handoff method, and refund boundary are confirmed in writing.
What is the refund boundary?+
A full refund is available before review work starts. If the promised review is not delivered within the agreed window, you may choose a full refund. Completed review work is otherwise non-refundable.
Will this make me win?+
No service can control an issuer’s decision. The deliverable improves organization and readability; it does not guarantee an outcome or represent a win probability.
