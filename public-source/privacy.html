PLAIN-LANGUAGE SUMMARY

No RebuttalKit account, processor login, case-data backend, advertising tracker, or automatic document upload. Paid-review files are handled only under an agreed scope and deletion date.

Commercial applications are closed. Public operator contact is not configured on this version.

1. Free workspace data

The application can store case fields, evidence statuses, notes, and timeline events in local browser storage on the device and browser profile you use. RebuttalKit does not operate an application server that receives those case entries.

JSON, text, and PDF outputs are created for you to save. You control where those exports are stored and shared.

2. Compatibility check

The founder-review form creates a short request in your browser. It does not upload documents or submit data automatically. If applications are open and you choose “Open in email,” your own email application and provider handle the message.

Do not include sensitive case material

The compatibility request should not contain customer names, card data, transaction IDs, credentials, evidence files, or private communications.

3. Accepted founder review

Documents are requested only after the case is accepted and the scope, secure handoff, turnaround, refund rule, and deletion date are confirmed in writing. The preferred handoff is a customer-controlled folder restricted to the published review address.

The reviewer may create working copies needed to prepare the deliverable. Working copies are deleted within seven days after delivery or the included correction round, whichever is later, unless a shorter period is agreed. This promise does not cover originals or provider copies controlled by you.

Customer files are not placed in GitHub, public issue trackers, marketing analytics, or unrelated AI services without explicit informed permission.

4. Payment information

Checkout is hosted by the configured payment provider. RebuttalKit does not ask you to send card or bank details by email, chat, or the application. The provider processes payment and receipt information under its own privacy terms.

5. Website delivery

GitHub Pages serves the static site and may process ordinary request information such as IP address, browser details, requested URL, and timestamps under GitHub’s practices. RebuttalKit does not add product analytics, advertising pixels, or behavioral tracking to the application.

The service worker can cache public interface files for offline use. Remove the installed app or clear site data to remove that cache.

6. Your controls

  • Export a local backup before clearing browser data.
  • Use Reset or browser controls to delete the current local case.
  • Revoke access to a customer-controlled review folder.
  • Request correction or deletion of review correspondence from the published review address, subject to payment, fraud-prevention, and legal record obligations.

RebuttalKit cannot recover local data after it is deleted and has no case-account backup.

7. Security limits

Local-first storage reduces server exposure but cannot protect a compromised, shared, or unlocked device. Use a trusted device, updated software, and a private browser profile. Do not send credentials or unnecessary sensitive information.

If private information is misdirected or exposed, intake and checkout are paused while access is contained and applicable notification duties are assessed.

8. Contact and changes

The public review address and operator identity appear only when commercial operations are configured. Material privacy changes will be reflected on this page with a new version date. Local law may provide additional rights.

This page describes product operations and is not legal advice.
