Choose evidence by sale type

  • Physical: tracking, destination, item record
  • Digital: delivery, download, access log
  • Service: scope, attendance, work product
  • Subscription: consent, terms, account usage

Do not submit a generic evidence dump

Start with the chargeback reason shown in the live Shopify admin, then include the smallest set of records that answers it. A carrier record may be central to a physical non-receipt case but irrelevant to a duplicate-charge case.

Physical order packet

  • Order and payment identifiers
  • Product, SKU, quantity, and destination
  • Carrier tracking and delivered status
  • Address consistency and any delivery photo
  • Customer contact about delivery, return, or replacement

Digital or service packet

  • Listing, scope, or purchase terms
  • Timestamped delivery or account-access record
  • Download, login, attendance, or completion record
  • Customer acknowledgment, use, or revision request
  • Refund and cancellation handling

Final portal checks

Shopify, processors, issuers, and card networks can change requirements. Confirm the current deadline, reason, file formats, size limits, and any evidence prompts in the live admin. RebuttalKit is an independent organization tool and is not affiliated with Shopify.
