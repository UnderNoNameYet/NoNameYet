WORKING RULES

Keep the tool factual.

DO
  • Copy exact dates and identifiers
  • Keep original source files
  • Mark genuine gaps as missing
  • Use neutral, specific language
  • Verify the live portal before submitting
DO NOT
  • Guess the customer’s intent
  • Alter, backdate, or fabricate proof
  • Paste unnecessary sensitive data
  • Treat readiness as a win probability
  • Assume processor rules never change
