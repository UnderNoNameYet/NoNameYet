# RebuttalKit Launch Demo — 60-Second Storyboard

## Recording rules

- Use only the built-in fictional demo and the fictional review sample.
- Record at 1920×1080, 30 fps, browser zoom 100%, with notifications hidden.
- Keep the pointer deliberate; avoid rapid scrolling or decorative cursor circles.
- Use captions even if narration is recorded.
- Never show a real inbox, payment dashboard, customer folder, personal browser profile, or processor account.
- Do not claim a win rate, processor endorsement, legal advice, AI verification, or guaranteed revenue.
