# TenantProof ten-day marketing experiment

Status: plan only. No outreach is authorized by this document.

## Objective

Test whether one qualified Supabase SaaS founder will purchase a bounded tenant-isolation verification. The revenue objective is at least USD 100 within ten days after launch; it is an experiment target, not a guarantee.

## Ideal customer profile

- Multi-tenant SaaS using Supabase/PostgreSQL
- Built or accelerated with Lovable, Bolt, Replit, Cursor, or Next.js
- Preparing to onboard paying users or responding to an authorization concern
- At least two customer organizations or meaningful sub-roles
- Can provide staging or a dedicated test project
- Has authority to commission the review

Exclude generic brochure sites, single-user products, unsupported databases, requests to test third parties, and buyers seeking certification.

## Offer wedge

> We use two synthetic tenants to show whether Customer A can read or change Customer B's in-scope data. You receive the executed matrix, redacted evidence, and—on the repair package—a reviewable patch and regression checks.

Do not lead with “security audit.” Lead with the exact boundary and deliverable.

## Channel order

1. **Current paid requests:** individually apply to recent Supabase RLS, authorization, or pre-launch review jobs where location and qualifications permit.
2. **Explicit public founder pain:** reply only where the founder is asking for help with tenant isolation, RLS, or launch verification.
3. **Warm technical communities:** share the fictional matrix as an educational artifact, not as a disguised testimonial.
4. **Direct outreach:** only to public business contacts where the app's multi-tenant Supabase architecture and current trigger are genuinely evident; no personal mailbox and no bulk sends.

## Ten-day sequence

- **Day 0:** publish request-only site and verify intake.
- **Day 1:** submit up to three tailored paid-job proposals.
- **Day 2:** respond to up to two explicit public requests; publish one concise explanation of why `RLS enabled` is not a tenant-boundary test.
- **Day 3:** review opens, replies, objections, and qualification failures.
- **Day 4:** refine only the message or package boundary—not the whole product.
- **Days 5–7:** contact up to five additional highly matched prospects.
- **Days 8–9:** hold qualification calls or answer technical questions; issue an invoice only after written scope.
- **Day 10:** record outcomes and apply the stop rule.

Maximum initial outbound set: ten well-matched buyers. Do not increase volume to compensate for weak response.

## Message structure

1. The exact trigger observed.
2. One boundary question relevant to their role model.
3. The concrete evidence artifact.
4. Staging-first and no-public-secrets safeguard.
5. A low-friction request to confirm fit.

Never claim to have tested the prospect, found a vulnerability, served a client, or improved a business result without authorization and evidence.

## Metrics

- Qualified prospects contacted
- Replies
- Serious technical conversations
- Scopes requested
- Written authorizations
- Invoices issued
- Paid pilots
- Revenue received
- Primary objections
- Disqualification reason

Views and clicks are diagnostic, not success metrics.

## Stop and continue rules

Continue if there is one paid pilot or at least two serious buyer conversations with a credible path to payment. Rework or kill the offer if ten well-matched prospects yield no serious conversation. Do not respond by adding features, lowering the price blindly, or fabricating social proof.
