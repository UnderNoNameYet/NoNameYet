**Stop before review if any row above is incomplete.** Reconfirm a near deadline against the live processor portal.

## Source discipline

- Assign every supplied record an immutable evidence ID (`E-01`, `E-02`, …).
- Preserve originals; review copies may be renamed but never altered to change meaning.
- Separate facts shown by a record from customer assertions and reviewer inferences.
- Do not describe intent, fraud, authorization, identity, or delivery unless a record supports that exact claim.
- Keep adverse or conflicting facts visible in the exception list.
- Use only the minimum customer data needed for the accepted scope.

## 6. Final customer verification

The customer should mark each item only after checking the live portal and original records.

- [ ] The processor reason and deadline match the portal.
- [ ] Every date, amount, name, and identifier matches an original record.
- [ ] Every material statement points to evidence included in the packet.
- [ ] No record was fabricated, backdated, cropped deceptively, or altered to change meaning.
- [ ] Redactions remove unnecessary data without hiding relevant context.
- [ ] Filenames, order, types, and combined size fit current portal requirements.
- [ ] The response addresses this dispute reason without motive claims or legal conclusions.
- [ ] The customer understands that the processor or issuer decides independently.
- [ ] The customer—not the reviewer—will perform the final submission.
